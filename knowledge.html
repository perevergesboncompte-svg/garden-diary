Bok Choy

shallow · fits, shallow rooted

Carrot

intermediate · marginal, short or round varieties only (Paris Market, Thumbelina, Chantenay), full-length types need 12

Chard

shallow · fits, at 8 inches it is near your floor so water more often

Cilantro

shallow · fits, but it is a resow crop not a long-stay one
