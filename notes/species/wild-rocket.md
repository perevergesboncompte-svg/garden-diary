# Wild Rocket

- Botanical name: Diplotaxis tenuifolia [Botanical Interests]
- Family: Brassicaceae [hand-entered]
- Not the same plant as salad arugula: salad rocket is Eruca, a different genus, so the figures in arugula.md do not transfer to this crop [hand-entered]

## Sowing
- Sowing depth inches: a quarter [Botanical Interests]
- Seed spacing: a group of 6 seeds every 6 in, thinned to one every 6 in once seedlings are half an inch tall [Botanical Interests]
- Row spacing: not stated, with coverage given instead as 280 ft a packet [Botanical Interests]
- Germination window days: 10 to 15 [Botanical Interests]
- Transplanting: starting indoors is not recommended [Botanical Interests]
- Succession interval weeks: 3 [Botanical Interests]
- Seeds per packet: about 1,600, which sows far more row than a small garden needs [Botanical Interests]
- Local sowing months: not covered by any UC source reached, since neither UC chart carries a rocket row

## Growing
- Days to harvest: 30 to 50 [Botanical Interests]
- Life cycle: perennial, unlike salad arugula which is an annual [Botanical Interests]
- Plant height inches: 12 to 24 [Botanical Interests]
- Flavour against other rocket: slightly stronger and more complex, and the plant is slower growing [Botanical Interests]
- Sun: full sun [Botanical Interests]
- Tolerance of part shade: not stated
- Bolting threshold: not stated, and no heat tolerance claim is made
- Cold: rated cold tolerant and frost tolerant [Botanical Interests]
- Target soil pH: not stated
- Crop coefficient: 1.0 [hand-entered, small-vegetable class after FAO-56 Table 12, cited not copied]

- Minimum soil depth: not stated

## Soil and rotation

## Harvest
- Repeat harvest: pick leaves individually, or cut the whole plant off at ground level [Botanical Interests]
- Harvest stage: as baby greens or grown on to full size [Botanical Interests]
- Edible flowers: yes [Botanical Interests]
- Being perennial changes the bed plan: it holds its ground between seasons where an annual clears out, so it suits a permanent strip or a pot rather than a rotated block [hand-entered]
- Spreading or self-seeding: not stated by the vendor, which is a gap worth watching given the genus is a wild plant
