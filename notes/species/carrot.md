# Carrot

- Botanical name: Daucus carota [EcoCrop]
- Family: Umbelliferae [EcoCrop]

## Sowing
- Sowing depth mm: 6 [hand-entered]
- Row spacing cm: 20 [OpenFarm]
- Plant spacing cm: 5 [OpenFarm]
- Days to germination: 12 [PlantVarietyDB, median of 16 varieties]
- Germination window days: 10 to 21 [openplantdb, median of 50 varieties, hand estimate was 14 to 21]
- Germination soil temperature F: 45 to 75 [openplantdb, median of 50 varieties]
- Local sowing months: year round, June to August second best [UC Master Gardeners Orange County]
- Succession interval days: 21 [hand-entered]

## Growing
- Days to harvest from seed: 65 to 75 [openplantdb, median of 50 varieties sown direct, not transplanted]
- Field cycle days: 40 to 150 [EcoCrop, whole-field cycle, longer than days to first pick]
- Optimal temperature F: 59 to 75 [EcoCrop]
- Absolute temperature F: 37 to 86 [EcoCrop]
- Killed below F: 32 [EcoCrop]
- Sun: Full Sun [OpenFarm]
- Target soil pH: 6.0, lime below 5.5 [Mid-Atlantic Commercial Vegetable Recommendations. In a high-organic raised-bed mix above 6 percent organic matter, crops wanting 6.5 tolerate about 5.6]
- Soil pH: 4.2 to 8.7, optimal 5.8 to 6.8 [EcoCrop]
- Crop coefficient: 1.0 [hand-entered, small-vegetable class after FAO-56 Table 12, cited not copied]

## Raised beds and containers
- Root depth: intermediate [INRAE]
- Container depth inches: 8 to 12 [Maryland 8, NC State 10, Colorado State 12]
- Minimum soil depth inches: 12 [USDA NRCS, minimum effective rooting depth on restricted soils, which is an irrigation figure rather than a recommended box depth]
- Raised bed: marginal, short or round varieties only (Paris Market, Thumbelina, Chantenay), full-length types need 12 [hand-entered]
- Container friendly: 14 of 16 varieties say yes [PlantVarietyDB]
- Soil profile depth: medium (50-150 cm) [EcoCrop, field soil depth class, NOT rooting depth, do not read as a pot size]

## Soil and rotation
- Nutrient demand: low [INRAE]
- Rotation gap under cover years: 3 [INRAE]
- Rotation gap in open ground years: 4 [INRAE]
- Do not follow: Apiaceae [INRAE]

## Bolting
- Cold is the main trigger: 32 to 50 F, best 35 to 43 F for 5 to 12 weeks vernalises this crop and it then runs to seed [MSU Extension]
- Warm days undo some of it: above 68 F reverses accumulated cold [MSU Extension]

## Harvest
- Repeat harvest: no, single pull [hand-entered]
