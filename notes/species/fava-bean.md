# Fava Bean

- Botanical name: Vicia faba [Botanical Interests]
- Family: Fabaceae [Botanical Interests]
- Also called: broad bean [hand-entered]

## Sowing
- Sowing depth inches: 1 to 3 [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Seed spacing inches: 4 to 6, one seed per station [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Row spacing inches: 18 to 36 [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Wide bed alternative: about 1 sq ft per plant instead of rows [Johnny's Selected Seeds]
- Thinning: not required [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Germination window days: 10 to 15 [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Local sowing months: October to January optimal, and it is absent from UC's statewide table entirely [UC Master Gardeners of Orange County Seed Planting Chart]
- Mild-climate sowing guidance: sow early to mid-winter for a spring harvest, so an October sowing is early rather than late [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Another mild-climate figure: where winter lows stay above 10 F, sow August through September [Johnny's Selected Seeds]
- Transplanting: not recommended, because bean seedlings are sensitive to root disturbance, so sow direct [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Seeds per packet: about 18, sowing up to 9 ft of row [Botanical Interests, Windsor]

## Growing
- Days to harvest: 75 [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Days to harvest by a generic figure: four to five months, which conflicts sharply with the cultivar figure above and is recorded rather than reconciled [NC State Extension]
- Plant height inches: 24 to 48 [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Staking: needed only in high wind areas, because the stems are sturdy [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Grows best below F: 75, and it is exceptionally cold tolerant down to 10 F [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Ideal temperature range: the 60s F, with poor yield above the mid 70s, and it is called a poor choice where weather is dry and hot [NC State Extension]
- Sun: full sun, with no partial shade option offered [Botanical Interests, NC State Extension]
- Shade warning that covers this crop by category: avoid planting in shade the crops you harvest flowers, fruit or seeds from [NC State Extension vegetable gardening chapter]
- Nitrogen fixation: a property of the legume family rather than a figure any source here quantifies [hand-entered]
- Crop coefficient: 1.15 [hand-entered, dry bean class after FAO-56 Table 12, cited not copied]

- Minimum soil depth inches: not stated by any source reached

- Bed width note: at the stated 18 to 36 in row spacing an 18 in bed holds one row, and two rows only if that spacing is halved [hand-entered, arithmetic from the figures above]

## Soil and rotation
- Do not follow: Fabaceae [hand-entered]

## Harvest
- Repeat harvest: yes for fresh use, picking individual pods as the beans inside plump [Johnny's Selected Seeds]
- Pod size: 5 to 6 in, holding 3 to 8 large flat light green beans [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Harvest young to eat whole: pick at 2 to 3 in long and use like a snap bean [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Harvest for dry beans: wait until the shell turns hard and brown [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
- Cold and frost: rated cold tolerant and frost tolerant [Botanical Interests, Windsor]
