# Thyme

- Botanical name: Thymus vulgaris [Illinois Extension]
- Family: Lamiaceae [hand-entered]

## Sowing
- Light needed to germinate: no, germination is light indifferent, so the seed can be covered normally [Thanos, Kadis and Skarou 1995, Seed Science Research 5:161]
- Seed against cuttings: it can be grown from seed but is much easier from divisions or cuttings [Illinois Extension]
- Propagation listed by UC: stem cuttings only, with seed not offered as a method [UC Environmental Horticulture Notes 70]
- First year harvest: cut only a few stems in the first year so the plant can carry through winter [Johnny's Selected Seeds]
- Local sowing months: no UC source recommends a fall seed sowing, and the Orange County Seed Planting Chart has no thyme row to consult [UC Marin Master Gardeners for the absence of a fall seed window]
- Buy as a plant rather than seed: the practical conclusion from the sources above, since a seed-sown plant gives almost nothing in its first year [hand-entered]

## Growing
- Sun: full sun, and no source reached offers a reduced-light allowance
- Water: low, and this is the crop's defining constraint in a mixed planting [hand-entered, following the Mediterranean-herb guidance below]
- Wet soil shortens its life: in a wet climate most thyme selections last only a season or two in the garden [UC Master Gardeners of Napa County]
- Winter wet is the hazard rather than winter cold: poorly drained soil over winter shortens the useful life of many perennial herbs [UC Master Gardeners of Santa Clara County]
- Why a separate pot matters here: sharing irrigation with vegetables is what kills it, so its own container is the fix rather than a nicety [hand-entered, following UC San Luis Obispo on grouping herbs by water need]
- Target soil pH: not stated by any source reached
- Crop coefficient: not recorded, and it should not be borrowed from a vegetable class

## Raised beds and containers
- Container depth inches: not stated by any source reached
- Raised bed: poor choice in a bed watered for annuals, good in its own pot [hand-entered]

## Winter behaviour
- Chilling requirement: none stated by any source reached
- Invasive listing: absent from the Cal-IPC inventory [Cal-IPC Inventory]

## Harvest
- Cut fraction: remove about a third of the growth at any one time, the general rule for perennial herbs [Illinois Extension]
- Harvest timing: cut before flowering, because leaf production declines once it flowers [Illinois Extension]
