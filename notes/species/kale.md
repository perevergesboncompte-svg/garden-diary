# Kale

- Botanical name: Brassica oleracea var. acephala [EcoCrop]
- Family: Cruciferae [EcoCrop]

## Sowing
- Sowing depth mm: 13 [hand-entered]
- Row spacing cm: 45 [OpenFarm]
- Plant spacing cm: 75 [OpenFarm, for a full-size single-harvest plant]
- Cut-and-come-again spacing: 15 cm (6 in) for baby leaf. The 75 cm figure is for a full-size plant and does not fit a 21 in bed [hand-entered]
- Days to germination: 5 [PlantVarietyDB, median of 10 varieties]
- Germination window days: 5 to 10 [openplantdb, median of 39 varieties, hand estimate was 7 to 14]
- Germination soil temperature F: 45 to 75 [openplantdb, median of 39 varieties]
- Local sowing months: October to February best, March still works [UC Master Gardeners Orange County]

## Growing
- Days to harvest from seed: 55 to 65 [openplantdb, median of 20 varieties sown direct, not transplanted]
- Field cycle days: 60 to 85 [EcoCrop, whole-field cycle, longer than days to first pick]
- Optimal temperature F: 59 to 72 [EcoCrop]
- Absolute temperature F: 45 to 86 [EcoCrop]
- Bolts above F: 85 [hand-entered]
- Killed below F: 32 [EcoCrop]
- Sun: Full Sun [OpenFarm]
- Target soil pH: 6.5, lime below 6.2 [Mid-Atlantic Commercial Vegetable Recommendations. In a high-organic raised-bed mix above 6 percent organic matter, crops wanting 6.5 tolerate about 5.6]
- Soil pH: 4.5 to 8.3, optimal 5.4 to 6 [EcoCrop]
- Crop coefficient: 1.0 [hand-entered, small-vegetable class after FAO-56 Table 12, cited not copied]

## Raised beds and containers
- Root depth: intermediate [INRAE]
- Container depth inches: 8 to 10 [NC State 8, Colorado State 10]
- Minimum soil depth inches: 12 [USDA NRCS, minimum effective rooting depth on restricted soils, which is an irrigation figure rather than a recommended box depth]
- Raised bed: fits, 8 to 10 inches is exactly your depth, so no margin [hand-entered]
- Container friendly: 9 of 10 varieties say yes [PlantVarietyDB]
- Soil profile depth: shallow (20-50 cm) [EcoCrop, field soil depth class, NOT rooting depth, do not read as a pot size]

## Soil and rotation
- Nutrient demand: high [INRAE]
- Rotation gap under cover years: 3 [INRAE]
- Rotation gap in open ground years: 4 [INRAE]
- Avoid these soils: acid [INRAE]
- Do not follow: Brassicaceae [INRAE]

## Bolting
- Cold is the main trigger: 32 to 55 F, best 43 F for 3 to 5 weeks vernalises this crop and it then runs to seed [MSU Extension]
- Nothing reverses it once vernalised [MSU Extension]

## Harvest
- Repeat harvest: lowest leaves upward, 1 to 2 years [hand-entered]
