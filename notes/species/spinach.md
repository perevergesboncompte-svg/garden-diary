# Spinach

- Botanical name: Spinacia oleracea [EcoCrop]
- Family: Chenopodiaceae [EcoCrop]

## Sowing
- Sowing depth mm: 13 [NC State Extension gives quarter to half an inch, which this matches]
- Row spacing cm: 30 [OpenFarm]
- Plant spacing cm: 20 [OpenFarm]
- Days to germination: 7 [PlantVarietyDB, median of 3 varieties]
- Germination window days: 7 to 14 [openplantdb, median of 28 varieties, matches the hand-entered estimate]
- Germination soil temperature F: 45 to 70 [openplantdb, median of 28 varieties]
- Local sowing months: October to February best, March still works [UC Master Gardeners Orange County]
- Succession interval days: 21 [hand-entered]

## Growing
- Days to harvest from seed: 40 to 50 [openplantdb, median of 28 varieties sown direct, not transplanted]
- Field cycle days: 40 to 120 [EcoCrop, whole-field cycle, longer than days to first pick]
- Optimal temperature F: 55 to 68 [EcoCrop]
- Absolute temperature F: 36 to 81 [EcoCrop]
- Bolts above F: 75 [hand-entered]
- Killed below F: 32 [EcoCrop]
- Sun: Full Sun [OpenFarm]
- Target soil pH: 6.5, lime below 6.0 [Mid-Atlantic Commercial Vegetable Recommendations. In a high-organic raised-bed mix above 6 percent organic matter, crops wanting 6.5 tolerate about 5.6]
- Soil pH: 5.3 to 8.3, optimal 6 to 7.5 [EcoCrop]
- Crop coefficient: 1.0 [hand-entered, small-vegetable class after FAO-56 Table 12, cited not copied]

## Raised beds and containers
- Root depth: shallow [INRAE]
- Container depth inches: 4 to 8 [NC State 4 to 6, Maryland 8, Colorado State 8]
- Minimum soil depth inches: 18 [USDA NRCS, minimum effective rooting depth on restricted soils, which is an irrigation figure rather than a recommended box depth]
- Raised bed: fits, needs 4 to 8 inches against your 8 to 10 [hand-entered]
- Container friendly: 2 of 3 varieties say yes [PlantVarietyDB]
- Soil profile depth: shallow (20-50 cm) [EcoCrop, field soil depth class, NOT rooting depth, do not read as a pot size]

## Soil and rotation
- Nutrient demand: moderate [INRAE]
- Rotation gap under cover years: 3 [INRAE]
- Rotation gap in open ground years: 4 [INRAE]
- Do not follow: Chenopodiaceae [INRAE]

## Bolting
- Cold is the main trigger: 32 to 53 F, best 41 to 46 F for 1 to 3 weeks vernalises this crop and it then runs to seed [MSU Extension]
- Nothing reverses it once vernalised [MSU Extension]

## Harvest
- Repeat harvest: outer leaves, 3 to 4 cuts [hand-entered]
