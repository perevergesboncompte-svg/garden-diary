### Carrot variety and layout set
- Action: planned
- Plants: carrots
- Details: The carrots are Tonda di Parigi, the round Paris Market type. Packet calls for
  1/4 in depth, 1 in between seeds, 6 in between rows, thinned to one every 2 in once they
  are an inch tall. That lays out as 4 rows across the 21 in width, 80 seeds sown and 40

  21 day germination. Cover the bed through that day or wait until it passes.

## 18.09.2026

### Sun exposure measured, setup recorded
- Action: observed
- Plants: all
- Details: Reported window is shade until about 13:30, then direct sun to roughly one
  hour before sunset. Measured against hourly Open-Meteo sunshine data: 4.0 to 4.9 h
  in mid-September against 9.0 h open sky, and 2.5 h over December 2025 on the same
  clock window. Part sun now, part shade in winter.
- Note: Exposure is afternoon-only, which is inverted from what cool-season greens
  want. Heat and bolting are the standing risk, not light shortage. December is the
  binding limitation because winter is the productive season in this climate.

### Open issues flagged
- Action: observed
- Plants: cilantro, parsley, dill
- Details: All three sown together in one 8 in pot. Each has a taproot and each wants
  more depth than that pot has. Dill needs 12 in minimum and reaches 3 to 4 ft.
- Note: Resow into separate space rather than transplanting, since cilantro and dill
  both react badly to root disturbance. Cheap to fix at 3 days in.
