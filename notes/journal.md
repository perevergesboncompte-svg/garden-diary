## 18.09.2026

### Sun exposure measured, setup recorded
- Action: observed
- Plants: all
- Details: Reported window is shade until about 13:30, then direct sun to roughly one
  hour before sunset. Measured against hourly Open-Meteo sunshine data: 4.0 to 4.9 h
  in mid-September against 9.0 h open sky, and 2.5 h over December 2025 on the same
  clock window. Part sun now, part shade in winter.
- Note: Exposure is afternoon-only, which is inverted from what cool-season greens
  want. Heat and bolting are the standing risk, not light shortage. December is the
  binding limitation because winter is the productive season in this climate.
