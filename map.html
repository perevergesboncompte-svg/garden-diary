Garden map

Two balconies facing opposite ways, so each gets its own plan below. Marks in the beds are seeds at roughly where they sit; each pot carries one crop and one mark. Tap anything to open that planting.

Southwest balcony

Shaded until about 1:30, then sun until an hour before sunset. 4 to 5 h in September, 2.5 h in December.

Left to right: 40 Tonda di Parigi carrots in the left low bed, 42 Wando peas in the raised middle, spinach and lettuce in the right. Carrot marks show the stand after thinning, not the 80 seeds that go in.

South-east balcony

Morning sun, measured at 5.1 h in October falling to 3.7 h in December. Better than the southwest balcony in winter, but it never reaches the 6 h a full-sun crop is specified for.

Pots move between balconies freely, so these positions are a plan rather than a fixture. Thyme and oregano sit here because it is the sunnier of the two in winter. Basil comes indoors in November. The nine unlabelled pots are spare, and the suggestion for them is a succession of lettuce, green onion and radish sown two pots at a time every fortnight, plus one pot of garlic as a trial.
