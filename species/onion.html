Sets

Spacing decides bulb size

Row spacing inches

Why October beats February here
bulbing starts around 20 March regardless, so an October sowing builds leaves for about five months first where a February sowing gets about six weeks, and bulb size follows leaf number, with about 13 leaves the target UC Master Gardeners of San Diego County

Bolting

Bolting is driven by temperature once the plant is big enough
plants with a leaf base over about three eighths of an inch in diameter, exposed to 45 to 50 F UC ANR Publication 7242
Where this site sits
December, January and February mean lows of 49.3, 48.8 and 48.9 F fall inside that band hand-entered, comparing the profile normals against Publication 7242
A conflicting UC threshold
bolting occurs below 45 F for a sufficient length of time, which this site's winter mean lows stay above UC Master Gardeners of San Diego County
This conflict does not resolve
both figures are UC, and one puts the winter inside the risk band while the other puts it outside hand-entered
Daytime means are also in the vernalising range by one table
receptive at 4 to 6 leaves, optimum 48 to 55 F, maximum 64 F, over 4 to 12 weeks, with reversal only above 68 F, against local winter mean highs of 62.9 to 63.8 F MSU Extension
A documented fall-planting failure in a mild California climate
fall-started onions flowering the following spring and then unable to make a good bulb, with February planting advised instead UC Master Gardeners of Contra Costa County
The mitigation that follows from the threshold being about size
