Bolting

A flowered plant is a lost crop
once an onion, shallot or leek flowers it will not form a marketable bulb or stalk MSU Extension
Fall planting lowers the risk
fall-transplanted shallots are less likely to bolt than spring-sown alliums, because of a larger juvenile size requirement and longer vernalising time MSU Extension
