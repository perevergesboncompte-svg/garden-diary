Plan
sow 1/4 in deep. Keep it outdoors through October, then move it to a sunny indoor window. Pinch stem tips and remove flower buds as they appear. Resow every 2 weeks.

Notes

this is the one crop fighting the calendar. Cold stunts it and blackens the leaves at 50 F, and the local mean low is 60 F in October, 53 F in November, then 49 F from December through March. So it works outdoors for about a month and then has to come inside, which is only possible because the pot is portable.
