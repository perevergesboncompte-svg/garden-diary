Learnings

Cilantro is a succession crop, not a repeat-harvest one. Cutting it does not reset the bolt clock, so the supply comes from resowing every 2 to 3 weeks rather than from any one plant lasting.

Notes

Overcrowded. Three taproot herbs in one 8 in pot. Needs its own space or a bed. Germinates 7 to 14 days. Resents transplant, so resow rather than move.
