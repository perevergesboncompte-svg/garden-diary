two rows down the whole raised bed, 3 cm (1 in) apart in the row, against a 60 to 90 cm trellis or netting so the vines climb. The raised bed is the deepest root run you have, which suits peas better than the 8 in low beds.

Notes

Wando is a USDA 1943 shell variety bred for heat tolerance and also frost-hardy, so it takes the warm end of the local October-to-February window better than most peas. About 68 to 70 days to pods on 60 to 75 cm vines. Pick pods often to keep the vine producing. [variety note]
