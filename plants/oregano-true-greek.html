Plan
buy Origanum vulgare subsp. hirtum by name, not plain Origanum vulgare. Surface sow without covering, because the seed has an absolute light requirement.

Notes

rated for 2 to 6 h of sun, which is why it beats sweet marjoram on this site. Its own pot solves the water problem a shared bed would create. Rich soil costs it pungency, so do not over-enrich the mix. Replace about every 4 years.
