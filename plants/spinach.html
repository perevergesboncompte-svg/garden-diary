Learnings
The growing medium reads as a bark-heavy mulch in every photo rather than a garden or potting mix, which is worth knowing but is not currently causing a symptom. Measuring leaf colour in the photos puts spinach at hue 79.6 degrees against dill at 78 to 80, with near-identical RGB on the lit patches, so the two are the same green. An earlier reading of the spinach as pale and nitrogen-short came from comparing a shaded spinach leaf against a lit dill leaf, lightness in that one photo running from 36 to 57 percent. Colour judgements from a photo need a lit-to-lit comparison or a measurement, not an eyeball across two frames.
